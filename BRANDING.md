# GittyGo Branding Policy

GittyGo's source code is available under the MIT License. That license permits broad use, modification, and redistribution of the code, but it does not grant permission to imply that a modified product is an official GittyGo release.

The **GittyGo** name, logo, icon, and other distinctive product branding identify the official project maintained by Mohammad Bashiri.

## Allowed without additional permission

- Truthfully referring to GittyGo or linking to the official project.
- Describing a product as compatible with, based on, or forked from GittyGo, provided the description does not imply endorsement.
- Redistributing an unmodified official GittyGo release while preserving its license and attribution.
- Using screenshots or the name in reviews, articles, tutorials, and other commentary.

## Permission required

Please obtain permission before:

- distributing a modified build under the name **GittyGo**;
- using the GittyGo logo or icon as the primary branding of a fork or derivative product;
- using a confusingly similar product, package, application, repository, or domain name; or
- presenting any unofficial build, service, or organization as endorsed by or affiliated with the official project.

Forks and substantially modified distributions should use their own product name, icon, bundle identifier, state directory, and other identifying metadata. They may still state clearly that they are based on GittyGo.

This policy is intended to protect users from confusion, not to prevent truthful discussion or legitimate use of the MIT-licensed code. Questions or permission requests may be submitted through the official GittyGo repository.
